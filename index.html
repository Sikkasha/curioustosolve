Step 01 — Define

What is the problem you're facing?

Be specific. Describe the impact, frequency, and who is affected.

Step 02 — Explore Causes

Possible root cause categories

Problem:

Based on your problem, consider investigating these areas:

🔹 People: Skills gaps, communication breakdowns, unclear ownership
🔹 Process: Missing SOPs, approval bottlenecks, redundant steps
🔹 Technology: System limitations, lack of automation, integration issues
🔹 Data: Incomplete information, no tracking, delayed reporting
Step 03 — 5 Why Analysis

Drill down to the root cause

Ask "why" five times to peel away the layers.

1
Why does this problem occur?
2
Why? (answer to Why #1)
3
Why? (go deeper...)
4
Why? (getting to root cause...)
5
Why? (this is likely your root cause)
Step 04 — Improve

Suggested improvement actions

🎯 Short-term: Document the current process and identify the specific step causing the delay. Create a quick-fix workaround within 48 hours.
🔧 Medium-term: Design an SOP for the affected process. Train the team and assign clear ownership. Implement a tracking mechanism.
📈 Long-term: Automate repetitive steps, implement feedback loops, and establish a monthly review to monitor KPIs and prevent recurrence.
🔒 Control: Set up a control chart to monitor the process. Define upper/lower control limits and escalation triggers.

IT · Incident Management

Why did our server response time spike every Monday at 9 AM?

−65% Incidents
Problem Weekly 3-hour performance degradation affecting 400+ users, costing £12K in lost productivity.
Root Cause Scheduled backup jobs + batch report generation colliding with business peak hours.
Solution Rescheduled jobs to 2 AM, implemented resource throttling, and added monitoring alerts.

Ticket resolution time exceeded SLA 40% of the time — what was really happening?

SLA: 95%→99%
Problem 1 in 3 helpdesk tickets missed the 4-hour SLA, triggering escalations and customer dissatisfaction.
Root Cause Tickets were being routed to the wrong team 38% of the time due to vague categorisation rules.
Solution Redesigned ticket taxonomy, automated routing rules, trained agents, and added triage checkpoints.

Operations · Lean

A procurement process that took 22 days — reduced to 6 days with Lean principles

−73% Lead Time
Problem Purchase orders took 22 days on average — 8× the industry benchmark — causing project delays.
Root Cause 7 approval stages, most adding zero value. 60% of delays occurred at a single manager's inbox.
Solution Value stream mapping revealed 5 non-value steps. Removed 4 approval tiers, digitised forms.

Quality · Manufacturing

Defect rate of 8% on a production line — traced to a single machine calibration drift

Defects: 8%→0.4%
Problem 1 in 12 units failing quality check. £200K monthly rework cost. Customer returns increasing.
Root Cause Temperature sensor on Machine 3 drifting 2.3°C after 4 hours of operation — never caught by checklist.
Solution Added real-time sensor monitoring, automated calibration triggers, and updated PM schedule.

IT · Data Quality

30% of monthly reports contained errors — the data pipeline no one owned

Accuracy: 99.2%
Problem Board-level reports had material errors monthly. Decisions were being made on bad data.
Root Cause Three teams each assumed another team was validating the data. Classic diffusion of responsibility.
Solution Data ownership RACI matrix, automated validation checks, and a data quality dashboard.

Operations · HR

Why employee onboarding took 3 weeks when the benchmark is 3 days

Time: −80%
Problem New joiners waited 3 weeks for system access, causing disengagement and lost productivity.
Root Cause IT provisioning was triggered only AFTER HR closed the hire — a 5-day lag before work even started.
Solution Parallel processing — IT provisioning starts at offer acceptance. Automated Jira workflow introduced.
